How EnerCut AI works

EnerCut AI combines a Kienzle cutting-force equation with a Gaussian Process Regression model to estimate cutting behaviour for the selected tool grade. It evaluates the machining conditions in the experimental dataset, ranks the three leading options by estimated cutting power, and calculates spindle speed from the entered workpiece diameter. A fixed reference material removal rate of 1600 mm³/min is shown for consistent comparison across the recommendations.
